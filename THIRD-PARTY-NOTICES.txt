The user-initiated receiver setup builds the experimental UxPlay 1.74 source
with its internal mDNS implementation. UxPlay also contains separately
licensed components; their notices and license files are included in the
installed UxPlay documentation and in the pinned upstream source identified

For Windows reliability and app integration, the receiver setup applies
source patches that:

- select the intended IPv4 adapter for internal mDNS discovery;
- support RAOP-only advertisement for the optional AirPlay speaker mode;
- support a named Windows event for graceful receiver shutdown; and
- improve receiver reset/watch cleanup.

The complete patch logic is contained in scripts/Install-ReceiverEngine.ps1.

MSYS2 and multimedia dependencies
----------------------------------

The user-initiated receiver setup obtains the portable MSYS2 environment and
packages from the official MSYS2 repositories at https://www.msys2.org/ and
https://repo.msys2.org/. Installed packages include GCC, CMake, Ninja,
OpenSSL, libplist, GStreamer, GStreamer plug-ins, and FFmpeg/libav support.
These packages use multiple open-source licenses. The authoritative license
files installed by MSYS2 can be found below the receiver engine's
usr/share/licenses and ucrt64/share/licenses directories.

The current general-purpose MSYS2 FFmpeg build enables GPL components,
including x264/x265-related functionality. Installed license texts alone are
not sufficient for public binary redistribution. A prebuilt receiver release
must include or provide the exact corresponding source and build materials for
every conveyed GPL/LGPL component. MirrorSpeaker's receiver-package build
script blocks a public-release designation until a maintainer explicitly
supplies and confirms a corresponding-source bundle; that automated check is
not a substitute for a legal or license-compliance review. Until the
reproducible build, complete corresponding source, and explicit review are
all complete, the prebuilt receiver remains DO NOT REDISTRIBUTE regardless of
the script's metadata.
